Alder Creek Editions

About Us

Alder Creek Editions is an independent publishing company based in Georgia. It publishes fiction, nonfiction, and thoughtful commentary on topics such as recovery, family care, business, economics, and the systems that shape people's everyday choices.

Its collection includes several unique book series, including The Operational Reliability Series, the free Mile Marker Library of short recovery books, the large-print Sitting Room Series for older adults, The Paid Last Series of business books, and Küche, a children's series. The press also offers free worksheets and printables for readers to download.

Alder Creek Editions welcomes readers, reviewers, and booksellers who want to learn more about its publications. To explore its books, free resources, or contact the press about titles, publishing rights, and review copies, visit Alder Creek Editions.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What types of books does Alder Creek Editions publish?

Alder Creek Editions publishes fiction, nonfiction, and commentary covering recovery, family care, business, economics, and children's literature.
